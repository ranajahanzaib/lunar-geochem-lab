Methodology & provenance

The primary source is the Astromat Synthesis Compilation: Lunar samples v.1, February 2025 (DOI 10.60520/IEDA/113696). Its archive is CC-BY-4.0. The downloaded source archive is checksum-verified and preserved locally. Ingestion creates one analysis per source row, normalized DuckDB and Parquet tables, and a provenance manifest. Major oxides, trace elements, isotopes, methods, citations, and reported uncertainty fields are available where present in the source.

Mission and detection-limit fields are absent from the release and are never inferred. REE patterns remain raw because this source release does not include a vetted normalization table. Every plotted pair comes from the same source analysis.

Ti classes follow Neal & Taylor (1992), applied only to eligible bulk mare basalts: very low-Ti <1 wt% TiO₂; low-Ti 1–6 wt%; high-Ti >6 wt%. Blank cells remain missing. Populated values that are not numeric are retained as quality flags and excluded from numeric analysis; the source does not provide a separate censoring qualifier.
